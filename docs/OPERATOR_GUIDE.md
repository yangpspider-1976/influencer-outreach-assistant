# Operator quick-start guide

For outreach operators. You will spend nearly all of your time in one screen: the **Outreach
workspace**.

---

## The rule that matters most

**You send every first-contact message yourself.**

The tool prepares the message, opens the right profile and records what happened. It never signs in
to Facebook or Instagram, never types into them and never clicks Send. Before you send anything:

1. Confirm the profile you have open is the right creator.
2. Read the message you are about to paste.
3. Send it yourself, then come back and record the result.

If the account looks wrong, the profile is inactive, or anything feels off — use **Invalid account**
or **Skip**. Never send in doubt.

---

## Your daily loop

### 1. Open your queue

Sign in and click **Outreach workspace** in the sidebar. The badge shows how many records are
waiting for you. The workspace loads one creator at a time, ordered by priority, then due date.

If it says your queue is empty, everything assigned to you is done. Ask your campaign manager for
more work.

### 2. Read the campaign summary

The top card shows exactly what you are offering: client, location, visit period, deliverables,
compensation and the application deadline. If any of it looks wrong, stop and tell your campaign
manager — do not improvise different terms.

### 3. Review and personalize the message

The message box is pre-filled from the approved template with this campaign's and creator's details.

- **You may edit it.** A natural, personal opening line works better than identical copy.
- **Do not change** the compensation or deliverables wording.
- If you see a highlighted warning about **unresolved variables** (text like `{{first_name}}` still
  showing), fix it before sending. The tool will not let you mark the record Sent while tokens
  remain unless you explicitly confirm it is intentional.
- **Reset to template** restores the original wording at any time.

### 4. Copy and open the profile

Click **Copy message**, then **Open Instagram** or **Open Facebook**. The profile opens in a new
browser tab.

If your browser blocks the clipboard, the tool shows the text in a selectable box — select it and
copy manually. Nothing is lost. If the new tab is blocked, a direct link appears; click it.

> Copying the message and opening a profile **do not** change the record's status. Only you can do
> that, in step 6.

### 5. Send it yourself

In the Facebook or Instagram tab: confirm the account, open the conversation, paste, **read it
once more**, and send.

### 6. Record what happened

Come back to the tool and choose one:

| Button | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark sent** | You sent the message. Tick "I manually sent this message" first. |
| **Skip** | Not right for this campaign now. Pick a reason from the list. |
| **Invalid account** | The profile is gone, wrong or inactive. |
| **Duplicate** | Another record already covers this creator. |
| **Do not contact** | The creator asked not to be contacted. This blocks them in every future campaign. |
| **Save draft** | You want to come back to your edited text later. |

The exact text you send is stored for audit and reporting, so make sure the box matches what you
actually pasted before you mark it Sent.

Once the outcome saves, the next creator loads automatically. If the save fails (network drop), the
tool keeps your text, shows a **Retry** button and does **not** advance. Nothing is lost.

---

## Follow-ups

When you mark a record Sent, the tool schedules reminders (by default day 3 and day 7).

Open **Follow-up queue** to see what is due. Each entry shows the message you originally sent —
click **Copy previous** to reuse and adapt it, **Open profile** to launch the conversation, then
send it yourself and click **I sent it**.

Reminders cancel themselves automatically as soon as a reply, decline or do-not-contact is recorded.

---

## Recording replies

When a creator replies, update the record so the campaign manager can see the funnel:

`Replied` → `Interested` → `Negotiating` → `Confirmed`

Use `Declined` if they say no, and record any quoted rate in the notes so the manager can approve it.

## Things you may run into

**"Another operator is currently working on this record."**
Two people opened the same creator. Move on; it will return to the queue when they finish or the
15-minute lock expires.

**"This record was changed by someone else."**
A manager updated it while you had it open. Nothing was saved. Click **Refresh record**, check the
current state, then re-apply your outcome.

**"This creator is flagged Do Not Contact."**
They opted out. You cannot copy the message or mark it sent. Only an administrator can override
this, and only with a written reason. Do not work around it.

**Your session expired.**
Sign in again. Your edited message text is preserved locally where possible.

---

## What the tool will never do

- Log in to Facebook or Instagram for you
- Store your Meta password, cookies or session
- Read follower counts from a profile page (those numbers come from QROAD's own lists)
- Type, click Message, or click Send inside a Meta page
- Tell you a "safe" number of DMs per day — platform limits vary and are never guaranteed

If anyone asks you to automate any of the above, escalate it rather than doing it.
